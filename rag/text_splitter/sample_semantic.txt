Kubernetes CrashLoopBackOff means a pod is stuck restarting because its container keeps crashing.
Common causes include a bad image, missing env vars, failed probes, or the process exiting with a non-zero code.
Check kubectl describe pod, kubectl logs, and recent events.

S3 billing is based on storage used, requests, and data transfer out of the region.
Infrequent Access and Glacier cost less per GB, but retrieval and early-delete fees can add up.
A FinOps review usually starts with the S3 Storage Lens dashboard and lifecycle rules.
